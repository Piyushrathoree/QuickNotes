Why SQLite is everywhere

By Jo Park · 6 min read · Nov 3, 2026

SQLite runs on billions of devices. It's tiny, slow fast, & reliable.

Quick start

$ sqlite3 test.db
sqlite> CREATE TABLE t(a INT, b TEXT);
sqlite> INSERT INTO t VALUES (1, 'x');

Or in Python:

import sqlite3
db = sqlite3.connect(":memory:")
